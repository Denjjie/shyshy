SMART MANGROVE EXPLORER

Mangrove Park, Panabo City, Davao del Norte

Tourist Journey Stage: EXPLORE

Enhancing visitor experience through integrated smart technologies

01 · Tourism Problem

What Needs to Be Improved?

Mangrove Park in Panabo City has strong potential to offer an educational and enjoyable nature experience. However, two key problems currently limit it:

  • Unengaging visitor experience — Limited interactive ways for tourists to learn about the mangrove ecosystem, wildlife, and conservation practices. Visitors mostly rely on traditional signs or printed materials.
  • Inefficient data collection — Traditional paper logbooks are slow, often incomplete, and make it difficult for tourism managers to organize, analyze, and act on visitor data.
02 · Smart Solution

Three Integrated Technologies

A connected system that digitizes registration, delivers interactive on-site learning, and captures real-time feedback — transforming the EXPLORE stage.

🖥️ Digital Tourist Registration Kiosk / QR

Touchscreen or QR-based digital form at the entrance that replaces paper logbooks with structured, searchable data.

🤖 AI-Powered Digital Tourist Guide

Interactive chatbot + QR/AR points that educate visitors about species, wildlife, history, and conservation in real time.

📱 Smart Visitor Feedback & Monitoring System

QR-based digital feedback after the visit, feeding ratings and comments into a live management dashboard.

Technology 1

Digital Tourist Registration Kiosk

A touchscreen kiosk or QR code at the entrance. Visitors register digitally before entering — no more handwritten logbooks.

Data Collected

  • Name of visitor / group leader
  • Number of visitors in the party
  • Date and exact time of visit
  • Contact information (optional)
  • Purpose of visit (education, leisure, research, etc.)

Result: Faster entry, complete searchable records, and immediate data for tourism managers.

Technology 2

AI-Powered Digital Tourist Guide

A digital assistant / chatbot (via phone or on-site device) plus interactive QR codes placed around the park.

Visitors can learn about

  • Different mangrove species & facts
  • History of the park
  • Wildlife & biodiversity
  • Conservation practices

Practical help + interactivity

  • Activities & recommended spots
  • Safety rules & reminders
  • Directions to facilities
  • Short quizzes or AR content at QR points
Technology 3

Smart Visitor Feedback & Monitoring System

After exploring, visitors scan a QR code and submit feedback digitally in under a minute. Data flows straight into a management dashboard.

Visitors can rate

  • Cleanliness of trails and facilities
  • Condition and availability of amenities
  • Perceived safety
  • Quality of information provided
  • Overall experience / satisfaction

Managers see real-time insights instead of delayed or incomplete paper forms.

03 · Demonstration

How the Solution Works

1

Arrival & Registration

Tourist scans QR or uses the kiosk at the entrance and submits basic information digitally.

2

Access the Guide

After registration, the tourist receives access to the AI Digital Guide (web or app) with recommended areas.

3

Explore Interactively

QR codes around the park unlock species info, conservation facts, short quizzes, or AR content.

4

Give Feedback

At the end of the visit, the tourist rates cleanliness, facilities, safety, information, and overall experience.

5

Automatic Data

Registration and feedback are stored digitally — no manual counting of logbook entries needed.

6

Manager Dashboard

Staff see today’s visitors, peak times, satisfaction rate, and common concerns for better decisions.

04 · Smart Impact

How It Improves the Destination

👥 Visitor Experience

Interactive QR codes, AI guide, quizzes, and AR make exploration more engaging and educational instead of passive.

⚡ Efficiency

Digital registration eliminates most of the manual logbook work. Data is stored and organized automatically.

📱 Accessibility

Information is available on smartphones and can support simple language, multiple languages, or audio.

🌱 Sustainability

Less paper use + education on mangrove conservation encourages responsible tourism behavior.

🛡️ Safety

The guide delivers safety reminders, park rules, and emergency information during the visit.

📊 Destination Management

Real-time data on visitor numbers, peak periods, satisfaction, and concerns supports better planning.

Conclusion

SMART MANGROVE EXPLORER

Combines QR-based digital registration, interactive AI/QR/AR learning, and a smart tourism dashboard to solve unengaging experiences and inefficient logbooks.

Integrated technologies:

  • Digital Tourist Registration (Kiosk / QR)
  • AI-Powered Digital Tourist Guide + QR/AR
  • Smart Visitor Feedback & Monitoring System
